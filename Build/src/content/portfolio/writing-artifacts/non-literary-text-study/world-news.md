# World News

World-news articles frame events through selection, sourcing, order, and
language. I will identify the event, the sources presented, the people given
agency, and the context supplied to the reader.

I will distinguish reported fact from framing and consider how the headline
and opening establish the article's priorities.

Consider the angle, the selection of facts, bias, and the effect on the
reader's understanding of events.
