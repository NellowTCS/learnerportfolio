# Websites

Websites communicate through words, images, navigation, layout, interaction,
and technical choices. I will analyze the intended user, information
hierarchy, calls to action, accessibility, and the way design directs attention.

A website's interface is part of its argument: what is easy to find appears
more important than what is hidden or difficult to access.

Consider layout, navigation, multimedia, and how the design shapes the
message and audience.
