# Comics / Cartoons

Comics and cartoons create meaning through the interaction of image, text,
sequence, framing, and omission. I will describe how the reader moves through
the panels and how visual contrast or exaggeration shapes the argument.

I will also consider what the cartoon assumes the audience already knows,
because satire often depends on shared cultural or political context.

Consider layout, paneling, text vs image, caricature, and satire.
