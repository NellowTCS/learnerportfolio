# Pastiche

A pastiche imitates recognizable features of another text, genre, or writer. I
will identify the conventions being borrowed and explain whether the imitation
is respectful, critical, humorous, or transformative.

If I write a pastiche, I will explain which choices reproduce the original style
and which choices change its purpose.

Note the work being imitated and how the pastiche captures its style and voice.
