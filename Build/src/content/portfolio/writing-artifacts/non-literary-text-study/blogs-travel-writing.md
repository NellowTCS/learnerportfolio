# Blogs / Travel Writing

Blogs and travel writing construct a relationship between the writer and the
reader. I will analyze first-person narration, selected detail, tone,
organization, and the assumptions the writer makes about the place and
audience.

The important question is not only what location is described, but how the
writer's perspective shapes what the reader is allowed to notice.
