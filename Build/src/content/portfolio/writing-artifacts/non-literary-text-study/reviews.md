# Reviews

Reviews evaluate a work while also shaping the reader's expectations of it. I
will identify the reviewer's standards, evidence, audience, and balance of
description with judgment.

Tone matters because a review can persuade through confidence, humor,
qualification, or apparent fairness.

Consider the reviewer's evaluation, the evidence used, tone, and target
audience.
