# Diaries / Letters

Diaries and letters are shaped by private or implied audiences. I will analyze
the relationship between writer and recipient, the purpose of the document,
and the way personal details establish intimacy, authority, or distance.

The form can make a private observation appear historically significant, but
the writer's viewpoint still determines what is included and omitted.

Consider the intimate voice, the intended reader, and how the form creates
authenticity.
