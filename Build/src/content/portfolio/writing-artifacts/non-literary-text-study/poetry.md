# Poetry

Poetry compresses meaning into sound, image, lineation, and pattern. I will
analyze diction, rhythm, figurative language, speaker, and the effects of
line breaks or stanza structure.

The analysis should connect the poem's formal pattern to its emotional or
intellectual movement rather than treating devices as isolated features.

Consider speaker, form, sound (rhythm, rhyme, alliteration), imagery,
figurative language, and theme.
