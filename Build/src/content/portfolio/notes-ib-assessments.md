# Notes on IB Assessments

The assessments measure different kinds of reading and writing. They are not
interchangeable: each one asks me to make a claim, support it with specific
evidence, and explain how the evidence creates meaning.

- Paper 1: Guided textual analysis
- Paper 2: Comparative essay
- Individual Oral (IO)
- HL Essay (HL only)

For each assessment, I record the format, time allowed, assessment criteria,
and strategies that make the response precise.

## Working approach

- **Paper 1:** identify the text's situation and purpose before analyzing
  techniques. Each paragraph should connect a choice to an effect on the
  audience.
- **Paper 2:** compare how two works address a shared idea. Similarity alone is
  not analysis; the important question is what the difference reveals.
- **Individual Oral:** use a focused global issue and explain how both works
  construct it through form, language, and context.
- **HL Essay:** sustain one line of inquiry across a longer argument. The
  evidence should develop the claim instead of becoming a list of devices.

These approaches are starting points. The linked rubrics define the criteria
that I need to check before treating a response as finished.
