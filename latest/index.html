Neel Jaiswal’s Portfolio

IB Language and Literature

I am Neel Jaiswal, and I also use the name NellowTCS online. I am a nerd, a bookworm, a coder, and a reader. I like making things, taking them apart, and figuring out why they work. I also like reading because books can change the way people understand an idea, a person, or the world around them.

This portfolio collects my IB English work. It shows how I practice close reading, rhetorical analysis, and writing about the choices authors make. My goal is to explain not only what a text says, but also how it creates meaning and affects its readers. I want the pages here to be easy to find and honest about what I have actually done, not just what I was assigned.

I build software for similar reasons. A program, like a written text, is made of many choices. Its structure, design, and purpose affect how people use it. My public projects include browser tools, developer utilities, embedded device experiments, open-source libraries, and games. I usually start with a question and end with something that can be tested, changed, or used.
