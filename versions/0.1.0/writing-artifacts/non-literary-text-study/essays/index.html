An essay develops an idea through a sequence of claims, examples, and transitions. I will follow the movement of the argument instead of treating each paragraph as separate.

I will record the essay’s central claim, changes in tone, use of evidence, and relationship with its intended reader. The conclusion should show what the structure makes the reader understand.
