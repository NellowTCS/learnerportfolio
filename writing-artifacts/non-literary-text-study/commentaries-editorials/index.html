Analysis of commentaries and editorials.

Commentaries / Editorials

Commentaries and editorials present a position as an intervention in a public conversation. I will identify the writer’s claim, evidence, intended audience, and response to an opposing view.

The analysis should distinguish between information and persuasion. Word choice, concession, evidence order, and tone show how the writer tries to make the position appear reasonable or urgent.

Identify the claim, the line of argument, and the rhetorical strategies used to win over the audience.
